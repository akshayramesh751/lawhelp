DOMAIN: CRIMINAL_BOND

1. PURPOSE
This bond is executed for the release of the accused on bail.

1. Obligations of Surety
The surety undertakes to produce the accused before the court as required.

1. FORFEITURE
The surety is personally liable for all future acts of the accused and waives all rights to a hearing.

1. Jurisdiction
Subject to the jurisdiction of the competent court.

1. SIGNATURES
Signed and delivered by the surety.
