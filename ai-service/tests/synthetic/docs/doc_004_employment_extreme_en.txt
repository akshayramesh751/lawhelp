DOMAIN: EMPLOYMENT

1. ENGAGEMENT & DUTIES
The Employee is appointed as Software Engineer and shall perform backend development tasks.

1. Remuneration
The Employer shall pay a monthly gross salary of Rs. 1,00,000/-, payable on the 1st of each month.

1. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Employee agrees to keep company trade secrets secure during the tenure of employment.

1. Termination & Notice
The Employer may terminate this agreement immediately at its sole discretion without notice or compensation. The Employee must provide 90 days notice and forfeit all accrued benefits.

1. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and courts at Bengaluru shall have jurisdiction.
