DOMAIN: CRIMINAL_BOND

ARTICLE 1 - Purpose
This bond is executed for the release of the accused on bail.

ARTICLE 2 - Obligations of Surety
The surety must physically accompany the accused to the police station every day.

ARTICLE 3 - Forfeiture
If the accused fails to appear, the bond amount of Rs. 50,000/- may be forfeited.

ARTICLE 4 - Jurisdiction
Subject to the jurisdiction of the competent court.

ARTICLE 5 - Signatures
Signed and delivered by the surety.
